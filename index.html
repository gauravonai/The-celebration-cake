Our Story
Started from a home kitchen in Kothrud, now we serve all of Pune.

What began as weekend experiments with buttercream in 2019 turned into Pune's most-loved cupcake studio. We still do everything the same way — small batches, real ingredients, no shortcuts. Every cupcake is baked the morning it's delivered.
